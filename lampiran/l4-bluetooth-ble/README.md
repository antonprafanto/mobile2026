# Lampiran D: Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) & Integrasi Thermal Printer

Koneksi hardware smartphone dengan perangkat BLE dan pencetakan struk transaksi kasir menggunakan printer thermal ESC/POS.

---

## 🎯 Target Kompetensi & Sub-Topik

Setelah menyelesaikan modul ini, Anda akan menguasai:

- [ ] Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Architecture: Central, Peripheral, GATT, Service & Characteristic
- [ ] Scanning, Connecting & Data Transmission via flutter_blue_plus
- [ ] Protokol Printer Kasir: ESC/POS Command Standard
- [ ] Mencetak Struk Kasir, Barcode, & Gambar Logo via Bluetooth Thermal Printer
